    05 / Social style guide

    The Cosmic
    look, in rules

    What every post is made of. Everything here is the brand's own artwork, taken from the website files: nothing is redrawn. Click a colour to copy it, and recolour the artwork in any brand colour.

    Logo

    The comet wordmark leads. The Arlo mark is for small spaces: avatars, stickers, merch. Never stretch, recolour outside the palette, or set the name in a font.

    Colour

    Blue leads, cream is the ground, ink carries the type. The warm accents only appear small: on stickers and on Arlo. Click a swatch to copy it.

    Type

    One typeface, Archivo. Black caps for headlines, Medium for reading, ExtraBold italic for small labels. Type over the specimen to try a headline.

    07 / Voice

    How Cosmic
    talks

    Warm, capable, articulate, accountable, grounded. Talks to people as equals and never down to them. Short sentences. Facts over adjectives.

    Say

    • "Get a clear estimate."
    • "Same places. Different standard."
    • "We'll take it from there."
    • Real numbers: 500+ spaces, 98% on time, 5.0 rating.
    • Austin by name: neighbourhoods, streets, the heat.

    Don't

    • "Cheap", "handyman", "franchise", swearing.
    • Invented prices, discounts or dates.
    • Childish puns with the characters.
    • Where the founders come from: the work speaks.
    • Stock superlatives: "best in town", "premium solutions".
